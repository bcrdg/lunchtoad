Where the restaurants come from

Every restaurant on Lunch Toad comes from public information: business listings, review sites, and restaurants' own websites and menus. Hours, phone numbers and addresses change, and places open and close, so call ahead if it matters. We're not affiliated with any restaurant on the list.

The picks are random

The Toad picks at random from whatever towns and food types you've selected. Every restaurant has the same odds. No favorites, no paid placement.

Recommendations

When a pick comes with a recommendation, the wording tells you who's recommending it:

"[Restaurant] recommends…" means the restaurant told us that's their favorite.

"The Toad recommends…" means it's our favorite. Sometimes we pull these straight from a restaurant's own menu because we like them.

Coupons

Restaurants can send us coupons to show on the site. A coupon only counts if it's on your screen when the Toad picks that restaurant. Telling the cashier "Lunch Toad said a dollar off" won't work.

Restaurants: want to show up more?

If you'd like to bribe us so your restaurant pops up more often, send us something through the contact form. We'll happily take your money. We won't change the odds. The Toad cannot be bought.

Your info

Your town and food choices are saved in your own browser so they're there next time, along with one small cookie for a joke you may never find. Nothing is sent to us unless you use the contact form.
